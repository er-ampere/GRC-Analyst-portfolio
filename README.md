# GRC-Analyst-portfolio
Hi, I'm Amit Patel — a Field Service Technician with 5 years of experience in fibre, DSL, and IPTV, now pivoting into cybersecurity Governance, Risk, and Compliance (GRC).

I'm building this portfolio in public through Michael Lawrence's free 24-week GRC Mentorship Program, alongside studying for CompTIA Security+ SY0-701 (Jan/Feb 2027). My focus is telecom GRC — NIST CSF 2.0, ISO 27001, SOC 2, and carrier compliance including PIPEDA and CRTC obligations — turning hands-on network experience into risk, policy, and compliance work.

This repo documents that journey: landscape reports, framework comparisons, risk assessments, and policies, one week at a time.
